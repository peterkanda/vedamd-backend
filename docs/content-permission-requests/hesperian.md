Dear Hesperian team,

VedaMD is a free clinical decision-support tool for health workers in Kenya
and Sub-Saharan Africa, including community health workers. It works
offline on a phone and stores no patient data. Its content is released
free under a non-commercial licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), so anyone may
reproduce or adapt it at no cost. We believe this matches the spirit of
your Open Copyright Policy.

Your policy asks us to contact you before using Hesperian materials in any
digital format. We respectfully request permission to:

1. **Reproduce and adapt sections** of *Where There Is No Doctor*, *Where
   Women Have No Doctor* and *A Book for Midwives* in the app. Examples are
   danger signs, simple treatment steps and patient explanations. Each
   section would be credited to Hesperian, with your full contact details
   and a link.
2. **Translate adapted sections into Kiswahili** for patient-facing
   explanations. Translations would be reviewed by clinicians and shared
   back with you.
3. **Use selected illustrations**, where Hesperian holds the rights.

As your policy asks, we will distribute this material at no cost, allow
others to reproduce or adapt our versions without fees, credit Hesperian,
and send you copies of what we publish.
