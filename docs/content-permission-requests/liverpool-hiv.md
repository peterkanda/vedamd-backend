**Subject:** Non-commercial access to HIV and hepatitis drug interaction data for a free clinical decision-support tool

Dear Liverpool Drug Interactions Group,

VedaMD is a free clinical decision-support tool for primary-care health
workers in Kenya and Sub-Saharan Africa, where most people on ART are
treated in primary care. It works offline on a phone and stores no patient
data. Its content is released free under a non-commercial licence and is
never sold.

Your interaction checkers are the reference our users trust most for
antiretroviral and hepatitis C interactions. Your terms do not allow reuse
without consent, so today we only link to them. We respectfully ask:

1. Whether you offer a **non-commercial data licence or API** under which a
   free tool could show your interaction ratings (with the traffic-light
   category and a short summary), always attributed to you with a link to
   the full entry.
2. If not, whether you would accept **deep links** from our interaction
   screens to the matching pair on your site.
3. Whether you would be willing to **review** our own interaction records
   for the most common ART combinations in the region.

We will follow any conditions you set, including attribution, update
frequency and the removal of withdrawn advice.
