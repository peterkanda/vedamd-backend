**Subject:** Request for access to the product register and permission to reference SmPC content in a clinical decision-support tool

Dear Sir/Madam,

VedaMD is a clinical decision-support platform used by health workers in Kenya
to look up medicines, dosing and safety information. We want health workers to
see which products are registered in Kenya and to reach the Board's approved
product information quickly.

We respectfully request:

1. **Register data.** A periodic export (e.g. monthly CSV or Excel) of the
   public lists of retained/registered human medicines, suspended/withdrawn
   products and Emergency Use Authorisations, with the fields already shown on
   the public portal (registration number, trade name, INN, dosage form,
   country of origin, MAH, local technical representative, registration and
   expiry dates). We noticed the portal's public CSV export is restricted, so
   we have not collected this data automatically.
2. **SmPC and PIL content.** Permission to show short excerpts of SmPCs and
   Patient Information Leaflets published on the Board's website (e.g.
   indications, posology, contraindications, special warnings), always
   attributed to the Board with a link to the full document and the date
   retrieved. Until permission is granted we only link to the Board's documents.
3. **API.** Whether the Board offers, or plans to offer, an API or bulk
   data service for these lists, and any terms that apply.

We will follow any conditions the Board sets, including attribution wording,
update frequency and removal of withdrawn products. We would also be glad to
share our suspended/withdrawn-product alerts with the Board's
pharmacovigilance team.
